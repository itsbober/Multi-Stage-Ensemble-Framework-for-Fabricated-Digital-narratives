## Test Procedure

1. Give the participant 5-10 short news items or claims.
2. Ask them to predict whether each item is likely true or fake before using the app.
3. Run each item through the Streamlit prototype.
4. Record whether the model output helped the participant revise or support their judgment.
5. Capture suggestions for clearer wording, warnings, and probability explanation.
